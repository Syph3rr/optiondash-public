Accounts

Loading…

Every journal trade belongs to one account. IBKR accounts get their positions from TWS, and imported fills are filed under the IBKR account whose broker ref is the fill's account number (else the first IBKR account). In a Paper account the journal trades are the positions. Other accounts use a CSV of positions or the journal. Rename an account or change its broker ref in place; deleting an account with trades moves them to the account you pick. Move trades between accounts on the Trades page.

Settings are saved in this browser; accounts are saved in the journal.
