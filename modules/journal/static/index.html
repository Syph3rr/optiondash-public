TWS connection

This is the one TWS connection for the whole app: the analyser's market data and these fills share it, and the TWS chip in the nav (bottom of the sidebar) shows the same state. Read-only: nothing places orders. It never connects on its own; each press of Connect (here, in Settings or on the TWS chip) makes one attempt, and nothing polls in the background. TWS only returns executions from the current session, or up to 7 days if you raise Trade Log → show trades for in TWS settings. Older history is not available this way, so sync every few days. Fills already imported are skipped.
