Saved positions

An IBKR account shows its saved positions when TWS is not connected; while TWS is connected every read is saved automatically, so a CSV is only needed the first time or without TWS. An Other account with a CSV source always shows its upload.

  • type is stock, call or put. Stocks leave strike and expiry blank; calls and puts need both (expiry as YYYY-MM-DD).
  • qty is negative for a short. avg_cost is per share: an option bought at 4.35 is 4.35, not 435.
  • multiplier (default 100 for options), trading_class (e.g. SPXW) and account are optional.

Each account has one source: IBKR reads TWS (else its saved positions), Paper and journal-sourced accounts show their open journal trades, CSV accounts their upload. Broker positions are grouped by the account's open journal trades; what no trade claims is Not in journal. Avg cost is per share; Debit is what you paid to open a trade and Credit what you received, at average cost. Greeks come from the pricing engine with each contract's mark and solved IV ($ delta, theta in $ per day, vega in $ per vol point); unrealised P&L is measured from your fill, and options with no price or IV are counted as unpriced, not guessed.
